yfinance does not give you IV for individual NSE stocks. It gives you the last traded price of options — which is market data, not a volatility surface. To calculate actual IVR you need:

Real-time option chain premiums
Historical 52-week IV range per stock
Current implied vol calculated by solving Black-Scholes backwards
None of this is available via yfinance for NSE. The IV you'd calculate would be based on our Black-Scholes estimates using historical price vol — not market-implied vol. That's not IVR. That's HV (historical volatility). Making instrument selection decisions on fake IV data is worse than no filter at all.

What you actually have that's free and accurate:

India VIX. That's it. India VIX tells you Nifty 50's implied volatility. Use it as a market-wide IV proxy:

VIX > 18 → market scared → condors and spreads, not naked calls
VIX < 13 → calm market → debit spreads are cheap, buy them
VIX 13-18 → neutral → follow score as primary filter
This is a 2-line addition to morning_scan.py, not a full IV engine rebuild.
